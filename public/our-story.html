Our story

Aama Ko Bhanchha — a mother’s kitchen, kept warm for thirty years

━━ ◆◆◆ ━━

Traditional Nepali home cooking, served in a setting warm enough to stay, modern enough to linger.

Home cooking, done seriously

In Nepal, the best food in town often comes from somebody’s aama ko bhanchha, where dal has been on the fire since dawn. We built this house around that feeling.

Ghee where it belongs, Himalayan spices roasted in-house, rice from the hills and a clay pot of chiya.
