Everything guests usually ask before they arrive.

Reservations

Do I need to book a table?

You can request a table online, especially for a group or during festival weeks.

How will I know my booking is confirmed?

The booking form gives you a reference. Staff review requests and update the status to Approved or Declined.

Food & Spice

How spicy is the food?

Tell us your preference when ordering.

Dietary

Are there vegan and gluten-free options?

Ask staff which dishes suit your dietary needs.

Is the food halal?

Please ask staff for current sourcing and certification details.

Contact us for current directions and parking advice.

Are children welcome?

Yes, families are welcome.

Ask staff about current card, cash and digital wallet options.
